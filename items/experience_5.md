## Experience 5: Apple Tech Support

**What was it that you did?**

I worked for Apple Tech Support in a call center.

**When did you do it?**

From the ages of 23-25.

**What was good about it?**

It was nice learning how to do proper troubleshooting and case logging. It was genuinely interesting in a lot of aspects. Working with Macintosh computers was definitely better than phones.

**What was your favourite memory of it?**

I had so many customers genuinely grateful for my time and assistance, it was nice making a small difference in people's lives.

**What didn't you enjoy about it?**

The work load was brutal. 8 hours, every minute logged. You finish one call and 5 seconds later you're answering another non-stop every day.

**Give your experience a letter "grade"**

C+
