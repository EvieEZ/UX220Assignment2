## Experience 2: Rollerblading

**What was it that you did?**

I learned to rollerblade.

**When did you do it?**

When I was about 9 years old.

**What was good about it?**

It's been a wonderful way to stay active over the years and still rollerblade to this day.

**What was your favourite memory of it?**

Rollerblading through a forest trail in Brantford, I came across a herd of deer.

**What didn't you enjoy about it?**

I fell pretty badly when I was younger and got road rash all around my shoulder.

**Give your experience a letter "grade"**

A+
