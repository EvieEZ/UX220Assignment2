## Experience 1: Voice Exercises

**What was it that you did?**

I practiced and worked on my vocal muscles in order to be able to change my voice to many different qualities.

**When did you do it?**

I've been doing it for about ten years now and still ongoing.

**What was good about it?**

It helped get my voice to a place where I'm more comfortable speaking to people. It also makes me able to take on some amateur voice acting gigs.

**What was your favourite memory of it?**

I love people's reactions when I'm able to pull out an unexpected voice.

**What didn't you enjoy about it?**

Starting off was very rough on my vocal muscles, soreness and losing my voice was a risk that I often ran into.

**Give your experience a letter "grade"**

B-
