## Experience 4: Retail Work at Home Depot

**What was it that you did?**

I worked a retail job at Home Depot. I was a cashier and lot associate. 

**When did you do it?**

I did it from the ages of 18-23.

**What was good about it?**

It was a great way to stay in shape. I was lifting over five thousands pounds of merchandise on some days and walking over 10km in a single shift.

**What was your favourite memory of it?**

I loved being outside the store when there was a large snow storm and everything was quiet in the early morning. The look of the snow dancing underneath the lights.

**What didn't you enjoy about it?**

Terrible pay, terrible schedule, random hours and way too much work for how little I was getting paid.

**Give your experience a letter "grade"**

D
