## Experience 3: Cooking

**What was it that you did?**

I learned to cook.

**When did you do it?**

I went into learning how to cook in my early 20's.

**What was good about it?**

It was a great way to save money on ordering food and learning a skill with a wonderful immediate reward: eating delicious food.

**What was your favourite memory of it?**

The look of joy on loved one's faces when I cook them a delicious meal.

**What didn't you enjoy about it?**

The clean up is always the worst part. But its still worth it.

**Give your experience a letter "grade"**

A-
